# Licensing and Asset Log

To publish legally on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram, only use:
- Your own visuals and narration
- Music/sfx you own, generate with a license that permits commercial use, or that is explicitly royalty-free (e.g. YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, Free Music Archive CC0/CC-BY with attribution)
- Fonts with open licenses (SIL OFL)
- 3D models you build or that carry CC0 / CC-BY licenses (record attribution)

Do not use brand logos or die-shot photos of Intel/AMD/Arm/NVIDIA without checking their usage terms; redraw original illustrations instead.
